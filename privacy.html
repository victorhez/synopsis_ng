Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Paul Daniels TV ("we", "us") operates automated video publishing to YouTube and TikTok. This policy covers the app used to connect to the TikTok Content Posting API.

What we access

We request the minimum TikTok API scope needed to publish video content to the account(s) we manage (video.publish). We do not access, collect, or store personal data belonging to TikTok's end users or viewers.

What we store

We store OAuth access and refresh tokens for the accounts we manage, used solely to publish video content on our own behalf. These are kept as encrypted secrets in our CI/CD provider (GitHub Actions) and are not shared with third parties.
